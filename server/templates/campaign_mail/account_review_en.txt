You created an account on OpenSankey. Whether you use it regularly or never came

It is not quite the same tool anymore:

  - It no longer just draws, it computes. OpenSankey reconciles your data: you
    enter what you know, with its uncertainties and its intervals, and the solver
    works out the rest by closing the balances. Stocks are part of the material
    balance. It is a flow analysis tool now, not just a figure tool.

  - It does not lock you in. Your data stays in Excel, both ways: import, fix
    things in the spreadsheet, export again. Nothing you build here is trapped
    inside the tool. (SankeyMATIC and STAN files open too, by the way.)

  - It produces a deliverable, not just a picture. A diagram, or a whole
    portfolio, publishes as a standalone browsable website — to send to a client,
    to put online, to embed in an existing page.

  - It no longer fights you over placement. Automatic layout, locking for the
    nodes you want fixed, aggregation levels. You decide, the tool handles the
    rest.

Also: interface and documentation in five languages, built-in guided tour.

It is not quite the same tool anymore

  • It no longer just draws, it computes. OpenSankey reconciles your data: you enter what you know, with its uncertainties and its intervals, and the solver works out the rest by closing the balances. Stocks are part of the material balance. It is a flow analysis tool now, not just a figure tool.
  • It does not lock you in. Your data lives in Excel, both ways. Your old SankeyMATIC or STAN work opens directly. What you build here, you can take back out.
  • It produces a deliverable, not just a picture. A diagram, or a whole portfolio, publishes as a standalone browsable website — to send to a client, to put online, to embed in an existing page.
  • It no longer fights you over placement. Automatic layout, locking for the nodes you want fixed, aggregation levels. You decide, the tool handles the rest.

Also: interface and documentation in five languages, built-in guided tour.
